Sticky for Google Tasks

A macOS app by SYDD Studio

What the app does

Sticky for Google Tasks shows your Google Tasks list in a small, translucent, always-on-top window on your Mac, so your to-do list stays visible while you work in other apps. It is a desktop companion to Google Tasks: the same tasks you see at tasks.google.com, in Gmail, and in the Google Tasks mobile app.

  • Always on top: the window floats above other apps, with adjustable opacity.
  • Two-way sync: changes made in the app appear in Google Tasks, and changes made anywhere else appear in the app.
  • Full task editing: add, rename, reorder, indent into subtasks, add details, complete, and delete tasks, with undo.
  • Restore deleted tasks: the app can list and restore tasks you have deleted.
  • Menu bar app: it lives in the macOS menu bar and stays out of the Dock.

The app is distributed for macOS. Availability: Mac App Store (coming soon).

How the app uses Google user data

With your permission, the app requests one Google OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks, to read and modify your Google Tasks task lists and tasks. This data is used for a single purpose: to display and edit your tasks inside the app's window on your Mac.

  • You sign in through your web browser using Google's standard OAuth 2.0 flow; the app never sees your password.
  • Requests go directly from your Mac to Google's Tasks API. There is no SYDD Studio server, no analytics, and no third-party service.
  • The developer has no access to your tasks or your account.
